Content creation and publishing for Mona Moon

Mona Moon Content is a web-based content management application designed to support the creation, organization and publishing of lifestyle, fashion and fitness content for the Mona Moon digital brand.

About Mona Moon Content

The application provides a centralized workflow for preparing social media content. It helps organize content ideas, captions, media assets and publishing activities while maintaining a consistent digital brand.

Core Features

Content Creation

Prepare and organize lifestyle, fashion and fitness content for social media publishing.

Content Management

Manage content ideas, captions and media assets through a structured publishing workflow.

Social Publishing

Support authorized publishing workflows to connected social platforms, including TikTok.

How It Works

Content is prepared and reviewed as part of the Mona Moon Content workflow. When publishing is requested, the application uses authorized platform integrations to send approved content to the selected social media account. Users retain control over what content is published.

Purpose

Mona Moon Content is operated for the Mona Moon digital brand and is intended to simplify its content production and social media publishing workflow.
