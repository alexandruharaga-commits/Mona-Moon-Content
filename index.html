About

About Mona Moon Content

Mona Moon Content provides a centralized workflow for preparing social media content. The application helps organize content ideas, captions, media assets, publishing activities and platform-specific content while maintaining a consistent digital brand identity.


The application is designed to support the Mona Moon content workflow from initial content preparation through review and authorized publishing to supported social media platforms.

Workflow

How It Works

Mona Moon Content follows a structured workflow designed to keep content preparation and publishing organized.

1. Prepare

Content ideas, captions and media assets are prepared for the Mona Moon digital brand.

2. Organize

Content is organized according to platform, format and publishing requirements.

3. Review

Prepared content is reviewed before publishing.

4. Authorize

Platform access is provided through the official authorization mechanisms offered by supported social media services.

5. Publish

Approved content can then be transferred or published using the supported platform integration.

Integrations

Supported Platforms

Facebook

Content planning supports the broader Mona Moon social media presence, including Facebook.

Transparency

Privacy & User Control

Mona Moon Content only uses platform access that has been explicitly authorized through the relevant platform's official authorization process. Platform permissions are used only for the functionality associated with the content workflow.
