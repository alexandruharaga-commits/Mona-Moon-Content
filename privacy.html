Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Mona Moon Content respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users interact with Mona Moon Content and when the application connects to supported social media platforms.

1. About Mona Moon Content

Mona Moon Content is a content management and publishing application used to organize, review and publish social media content for the Mona Moon digital brand.

2. Information We May Process

When a user chooses to connect a supported social media account, Mona Moon Content may process information made available through that platform's official authorization process.

This may include:

  • Basic account information authorized by the user.
  • Platform permissions required for enabled application features.
  • Content and media selected for publishing.
  • Technical information required to complete authorization and publishing requests.

3. TikTok Integration

When Mona Moon Content is connected to TikTok, access is requested through TikTok's official authorization process. The application may access only the information and permissions that the user authorizes.

Authorized TikTok permissions are used to support the application's account connection and content publishing functionality.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to:

  • Connect authorized social media accounts.
  • Prepare and manage social media content.
  • Support user-authorized content publishing.
  • Maintain and operate the application workflow.
  • Respond to support or privacy requests.

5. Sharing of Information

Mona Moon Content does not sell personal information. Information obtained through connected platforms is used only for application functionality and is not sold to third parties.

6. User Control and Revoking Access

Users remain in control of their connected platform accounts and may revoke permissions through the settings provided by the relevant social media platform.

TikTok users may manage or revoke authorized application access through their TikTok account settings.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality or meet applicable operational or legal requirements.

Users may request deletion of information associated with Mona Moon Content by contacting the support address below.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Mona Moon Content. Access to third-party platforms is performed through their official authorization mechanisms where applicable.

9. Third-Party Platforms

Mona Moon Content may interact with third-party social media services. Use of those services is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of the respective platforms.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, support requests or data deletion requests, contact Mona Moon Content:
